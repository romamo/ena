How Ena uses Jinius

Supplier

Ena Flour Mill Ltd sends the invoice on Jinius

Read

GET /v3/invoices/payable

Decide

The gate: pay, or ask the owner

Pay

From the BoC account, through bocAPI

Report back

POST approve, then markAsPaid with the bank’s reference

Live

Two companies on the Jinius integration environment: the bakery as buyer, and a supplier we registered, approved by Jinius. Its €180 and €1,240 invoices and a €119 credit note, read through the API.

Built and tested

Approve and markAsPaid, to the Jinius API 3.0 specification. When Jinius is offline, Ena reads its saved responses.
