Ena is the back office

A bakery, a barber, a photo studio: no staff for the paperwork, so the owner does it between customers. Four jobs:

Invoices

Paid one by one from a phone

Books

Done at night, or paid to an accountant

Cash forecast

None: cash is found short when a payment bounces

Loans

The instalment falls due in a lean month
