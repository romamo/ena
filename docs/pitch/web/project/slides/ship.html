How Bank of Cyprus ships it

Who it’s for

Micro-businesses and the self-employed who bank with BoC and have no back-office staff.

Who pays

A monthly subscription below the back office it replaces, or BoC bundles it into a premium business account.

What BoC gains

Instalments paid on time, Digital Business Loans sold the week they’re needed, payments that stay at BoC.

1 Pilot

Three months, business customers in one vertical. Measure owner hours and missed instalments.

2 Payment mandate

Production bocAPI with a standing payment consent, capped at the owner’s limit.

3 Lending API

The pre-filled Digital Business Loan goes from simulated to a real application.

4 Inside 1bank

Ena as a feature of the business account, with Jinius and JCC merchants as channels.
